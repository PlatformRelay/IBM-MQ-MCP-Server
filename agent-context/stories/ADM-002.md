# ADM-002 — Administer channels, channel authentication, and authority records

**Status:** Blocked by ADM-001 and ADR-0003  
**Authority:** `openspec/changes/guarded-administration/proposal.md`

## Outcome

Authorized profiles administer channels, channel authentication rules, and
authority records through the same typed, audited contract as ADM-001.

## Acceptance

- Create/alter/delete operations use validated domain types; no raw MQSC
  strings cross the port boundary.
- Security-sensitive objects (CHLAUTH, authority records) carry explicit
  destructive annotations and require exact target identity.
- MKurator-managed targets follow the approved pre-mutation behavior.
- Contract and live tests cover idempotency, not-found, conflict, and
  authority failure per object family.
