# SEC-002 — Payload-safe audit trail

**Status:** Blocked by ADR-0006 event-transport decisions  
**Authority:** `openspec/changes/remote-access-and-operability/proposal.md`

## Outcome

Every sensitive operation emits a structured audit event correlating
client/session, profile, policy decision, operation, target, outcome, and
latency — with no credentials or payloads.

## Acceptance

- Allowed and denied operations are both audited; POL-001 decision events are
  consumed here rather than defining a second audit path.
- Credentials and message payloads are structurally excluded, verified by
  test, not by convention.
- The audit failure policy (fail-open versus fail-closed per operation class)
  is decided, documented, and tested.
- A correlation identifier joins the MCP request, policy decision, and MQ
  call in emitted events.
